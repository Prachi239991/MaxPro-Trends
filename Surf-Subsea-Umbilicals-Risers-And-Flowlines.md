SURF subsea refers to subsea umbilicals, risers, and flowlines that are used in offshore oil and gas production.

What are the key components of SURF?
The key components of SURF are umbilicals, risers, and flowlines. Umbilicals provide power, control, and communication signals to subsea equipment. Risers transport produced fluids to the surface, and flowlines transport fluids from the subsea well to the processing facility.
