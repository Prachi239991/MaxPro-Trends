Electronic Scrap (e-scrap) Recycling Market Overview
The global e-scrap recycling market is experiencing rapid growth driven by escalating electronic device consumption, regulatory pressures, and increasing environmental awareness. The market encompasses collection, dismantling, processing, and reuse of electronic waste, including smartphones, computers, consumer electronics, and industrial equipment.

Market size was valued at approximately USD 50 billion in 2022, with projections indicating a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of around 7% from 2023 to 2030. This growth trajectory reflects both rising e-waste volumes and technological advancements in recycling processes.

Key regions include North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and emerging markets in Latin America and Africa. Asia-Pacific dominates due to high electronic consumption and less mature waste management infrastructure, but North America and Europe lead in technological innovation and ESG compliance.

Market Trends and Growth Drivers
Rising e-waste volumes: Global e-waste generation exceeds 50 million metric tons annually, with an annual growth rate of 3-4%. Urbanization and digital transformation accelerate waste generation.
Regulatory frameworks: Stricter regulations (e.g., WEEE Directive in Europe, R2 and e-Stewards standards) mandate responsible e-scrap disposal and recycling, incentivizing formal sector growth.
Technological innovation: Advanced shredding, sensor-based sorting, and hydrometallurgical processes improve recovery rates and reduce environmental impact.
Digitalization and Industry 4.0: Integration of IoT, AI, and automation enhances process efficiency, traceability, and compliance.
Sustainability and ESG focus: Corporates prioritize circular economy models, responsible sourcing, and transparent supply chains, boosting demand for certified recycling services.
