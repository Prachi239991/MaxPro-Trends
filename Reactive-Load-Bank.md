What is a reactive load bank?
A reactive load bank is a device used to test electrical power sources by simulating real-life reactive loads.

What is the purpose of a reactive load bank?
The purpose of a reactive load bank is to test the performance and stability of electrical power sources, especially inductive loads such as motors, transformers, and generators.

What are the different types of reactive load banks?
There are mainly two types of reactive load banks: inductive and capacitive load banks.

What are the key applications of reactive load banks?
Reactive load banks are used in industries such as manufacturing, healthcare, telecommunications, and data centers.
